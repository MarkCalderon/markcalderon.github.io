## Platform

web

## Users
- **Hiring managers and recruiters** filling senior frontend roles. They scan fast, usually from a CV or LinkedIn link, to judge seniority, track record, and fit.
- **Businesses wanting a site or web app built.** They look for proof of shipped work in a similar domain, then decide whether to make contact.

Both audiences weigh equally.

## Product Purpose
Personal portfolio for Mark Calderon, Senior Frontend Developer. It turns a visit into one of three outcomes, all of which count as success:
1. A recruiter or hiring manager reaches out about a role.
2. A business sends a freelance inquiry.
3. The site works as a credibility link attached to CVs, LinkedIn, and proposals.

## Positioning
- **Range:** 80+ WordPress custom themes and landing pages, plus enterprise web apps (bond lease management, logistics, document review, content delivery).
- **Cross-market delivery:** shipped for clients in the Philippines, Japan, Australia, and the US (New York production company SaaS); worked with Commude Japan teams; taught ICT in Brunei.
- **Teacher turned lead:** four years teaching, then team lead. Strong on mentorship, documentation, and explaining technical work clearly.
- **UI/UX plus architecture:** owns both interface quality and frontend architecture, including legacy-to-modern stack migration.

## Operating Context
Visitors arrive from a link (CV, LinkedIn, proposal, email signature), often on mobile, and leave after a short scan unless something holds them. Contact happens by email or LinkedIn. There is no form or backend.

## Capabilities and Constraints
- Static single-page site on GitHub Pages (`markcalderon.github.io`), deployed via GitHub Actions.
- React 19, TanStack Router, Tailwind v4, shadcn/ui components in `src/components/ui/`.
- Routes: `/` (portfolio). `/blog` exists but is unlinked and has no content yet.
- Availability: **open to both freelance and full-time roles**, while currently Senior Developer at Saphyroo (full-time, since Feb 2026).
- Home is a scroll world: CSS 3D camera flight through 12 scenes (intro, 5 projects, 4 roles, stack, contact) with condensed copy and an opt-in autoplay. No video generation, no tokens. Reduced motion gets the stacked page.

## Product Principles
1. Proof before claims. Shipped work and real track record carry the page; adjectives do not.
2. Serve both readers in one scan. A recruiter and a client should each find their answer within seconds, without a separate path.
3. One contact action. Email is the primary route; nothing that implies a form, booking flow, or response-time promise.
4. Every number and award comes from the record above. New claims need Mark's confirmation.
